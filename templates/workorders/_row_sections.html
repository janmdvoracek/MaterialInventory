{% load static %}{% comment %} The row sections of an entry form, one block per `sections` entry: JOB_SECTIONS on the job pages, REFUEL_SECTIONS on the refuelling pages. The row buttons are real submits (add_ / remove_) and need `formnovalidate`. „− odebrat řádek" is rendered `hidden` at the floor rather than omitted, so job_rows.js can unhide it. `data-unique-field` names the one picker per row whose choices job_rows.js hides once another row took them. A section with `sum_from` (Spotřebováno) also gets „= součet výstupů" (sum_), which fills its single row's `sum_field` with the other section's total: a screening job weighs only what comes out. It is `hidden` unless the section has exactly one row, and job_rows.js does the same sum in the DOM. A section with `mirror_from` (Použité stroje) has its `mirror_field` (tuny) kept equal to the other section's `mirror_source` total on every row that names a machine: job_rows.js live, the view on each rebuild (_mirrored_rows). job_rows.js and searchable_select.js are loaded here and are pure enhancement; they clone the row
